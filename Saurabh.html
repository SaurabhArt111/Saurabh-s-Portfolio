About Me
Open to Work

Designer by craft,
Artist by soul.

Hi — I'm Saurabh, a detail-driven Graphic Design fresher with a passion for visual work that is both purposeful and expressive. My journey began with pencil sketches and has grown into full brand identities, packaging, and design systems.

Great design is communication, not decoration. Whether it's a logo, a social post, or a hand-drawn portrait — I bring the same commitment: make it sharp, make it memorable, make it matter.
